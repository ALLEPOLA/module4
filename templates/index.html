Why use Machine Learning here?

Traditional forestry appraisal relies on simple lookup tables. Using extreme gradient boosted trees (XGBoost) allows the system to analyze complex interactions between structural parameters (density, moisture, taper) and real-time economics (bidder density, index trends) to produce accurate commercial estimations.
